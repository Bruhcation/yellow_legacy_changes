# Yellow Legacy Changes

Applies the **move changes** (PDF pages 11-14) and **stat changes**
(pages 15-19) from *Yellow Legacy by TSP* to the Gen 1 recompilation.

Try it: enable the mod, then check a rebalanced mon's stats in the
Pokédex, or fight with a buffed move (Solar Beam 180, Explosion 250,
Twineedle 40x2...).

## What's in

- **73 moves** rebalanced: power, accuracy and pp per the document's new
  values (deltas in the document are context; the mod sets the new values).
- **Type changes:** GUST → Flying, CUT → Bug, EGG_BOMB → Grass,
  KARATE_CHOP → Fighting, SLAM → Dragon.
- **Effect changes:** DIZZY_PUNCH 10% confuse; TRI_ATTACK and FIRE_PUNCH
  30% burn; ROCK_SLIDE, WATERFALL and SLAM 10% flinch; RAZOR_WIND and
  SKULL_BASH recharge like Hyper Beam; SKY_ATTACK and NIGHT_SHADE lose
  their special behavior; TRANSFORM gets +1 priority.
- **FOCUS ENERGY** is functional at exactly 2x the normal crit rate (the
  Gen 1 bug is gone), and **LEECH SEED** drains a flat 1/8 of max HP per
  turn (no toxic-counter glitch).
- **27 species** rebalanced base stats (Charmander line, Pikachu, Onix,
  Hitmonchan, Eevee, Porygon, and more).

## Notes

- Learnset/availability changes are not included — the document's pages
  11-19 only give move numbers and stats.
- PSYWAVE keeps its vanilla damage formula; only its accuracy is changed.
- Stat patches are partial: a stat the document does not change is
  untouched.
